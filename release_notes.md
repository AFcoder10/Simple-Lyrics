# Simple Lyrics v1.1.5

**What's New & Improved:**
- **Fluid Domino Lyrics Scroll:** Lyrics now cascade smoothly with a gentle wave and inertial spring physics as tracks play and scroll.
- **Refined Word-by-Word Sync:** Improved syllable grouping, smoother word pop-out, and frame-rate optimizations—plus a tighter `33` default font size.
- **Vibrant Morphing Background:** Dual counter-moving background layers with seamless continuous motion and smart highlight compression so lyrics stay readable over bright covers.
- **Smoother Transitions & Sync:** Added 3D-style bottom-slide transitions across Settings, faster lag-free full-screen artwork expand/compact, and tighter playback/seek sync.
