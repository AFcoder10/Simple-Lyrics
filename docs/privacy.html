Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Simple Lyrics. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our application.

2. Data Collection

Simple Lyrics is designed to respect user privacy. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not actively collect personally identifiable information (PII). The app operates locally on your device to synchronize lyrics with your currently playing media.

  • Media Metadata: The app accesses the metadata (song name, artist, duration) of the media currently playing on your device solely to fetch the corresponding lyrics and artwork.
  • Local Storage: We cache lyrics, artwork, and your settings locally on your device to improve performance and save bandwidth.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide lyrics and artwork, Simple Lyrics makes requests to external APIs. When doing so, your IP address and the requested song metadata may be visible to these third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party APIs.

4. Permissions

The app requires Notification Access to detect which song is currently playing on your device. This permission is strictly used for media playback detection and is never used to read your personal notifications or messages.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
